Built to be tiny

Everything here is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no frameworks, no trackers, no network calls after the page loads. The music is generated live in the browser. Sixteen hand-built cabinets sit beside a hundred and ten kit challenges and fifty standalone Atlas cabinets. Each Atlas cabinet is its own mechanic: there are no Classic/Rapid editions and no family variants. Every score is saved in local storage on the device you play on, and every finished run is filed on the leaderboard under whichever device account is signed in.
